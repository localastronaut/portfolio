After Hours Synth

A browser-based sound study that treats the interface like an instrument: tactile keys, slow envelopes, analog-inspired tones, and the mood of a late-night soundtrack.

Interactive study After Hours Synth
Sound as interface

An instrument that behaves like a mood board.

The synth blends simple interaction with slow attack, ambient trails, and a restrained visual surface. It is not trying to be a full production tool. It is a sketch for how code, sound, and atmosphere can meet in the browser.

The piece connects to the music and film work in the portfolio: sound is treated as texture, memory, and motion rather than only playback.

Build

Playable prototype.

The instrument uses Tone.js for synthesis, browser events for interaction, and a compact control surface for shaping the character of the sound.
