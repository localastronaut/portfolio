Expedition Provisions

Experiential World Coffee

Overview

Expedition Provisions is a concept brand that pairs specialty coffee with travel-inspired storytelling and tactile experiences. The goal is to celebrate global origins through pop-up tastings, field‑journal packaging, and a narrative e‑commerce flow.

Work included naming, identity, visual system, packaging explorations, and web comps for a lightweight storefront and event sign‑ups. The outcome is a cohesive design language and prototype materials ready for pilot activations and product tests.


Concept visuals for brand identity and experience, 2024.
