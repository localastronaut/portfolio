Crystal Nights

The Crystal Nights synth is a playable sound sculpture—part digital instrument, part interactive art piece. It invites users to explore ambient textures and lo-fi tones using a customizable, analog-inspired interface.


AFTER HOURS SYNTH




Use your computer's keyboard to play the sounds

Synthesizers carry a certain nostalgia—evoking memories of analog recordings, cinematic soundtracks, and lo-fi experiments from a pre-digital era. The Crystal Nights synth embraces this spirit, letting you explore sound as texture, memory, and motion.


Settings Overview:

  • Oscillator Type: Changes the basic shape of the sound wave—like sine for soft tones or square for sharper, digital-like edges.
  • Attack: Controls how quickly the sound reaches full volume after a key is pressed—longer attack feels like a fade-in.
  • Decay: Sets how fast the sound drops after the initial peak—used to shape the dynamics.
  • Sustain: Adjusts the level the sound holds while the key is held—low values give a pluck-like quality.
  • Release: Determines how long the sound lingers after a key is released—high values give more ambient trails.
  • Volume: Overall loudness of the synth output—set to balance with your headphones or speakers.
