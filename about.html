Who I am right now

I build intelligent products across research, code, and design.

I am Brandon Nelson. My current practice is focused on AI-native products: tools that understand context, help people make sense of complexity, and still feel crafted by a person with taste.

The interdisciplinary part is not a list of hobbies. It is the way I work. I use research to understand the system, software to make the idea real, and design to make the experience clear enough to trust.

How to read the range

The archive trained the current focus.

Music, film, photography, health tools, collaboration platforms, brand systems, and software experiments all taught different parts of the same discipline: how to build systems people can understand and feel.

Where I am going

I am most interested in intelligent tools that help people create, research, decide, and build. The work ahead is about agentic interfaces, creative software, and systems that make AI feel less like a black box and more like a usable collaborator.
