Current center

AI systems with taste and usable form.

The old work matters because it trained the range. The current work is narrower and sharper: intelligent tools, agentic interfaces, creative software, and products that need both research depth and a strong point of view.

Interdisciplinary, not scattered

Three disciplines, one operating system.

The through-line is not that I do unrelated things. It is that I use different disciplines to solve the same kind of problem: how to make complex systems understandable, useful, and emotionally legible.

01

Research the system

AI behavior, user context, workflows, health/product logic, and the questions a tool needs to answer before it earns trust.

02

Build the proof

Front-end engineering, prototypes, interaction models, product architecture, and coded experiments that make the idea testable.

03

Shape the experience

Interface design, visual systems, motion, story, and image-making so the product has clarity instead of just functionality.

This is where I am going

AI-native tools for people who create, decide, learn, and build.

I am moving toward products that combine intelligent agents, human-centered workflows, and creative control. The future direction is less about showing every medium I have touched and more about building tools that help people think, make, and understand faster.

Direction 01

Agentic interfaces

Systems that can reason with context, take action, and still expose enough control for people to stay oriented.

Direction 02

Creative intelligence

Tools for research, design, media, and storytelling where AI extends taste instead of flattening it.

Direction 03

Useful human systems

Products in health, education, work, and culture that turn complex information into interfaces people can actually use.
